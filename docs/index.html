Talking to the Shop

Four commands anyone in chat can use, and two reserved for mods.

!balance

Check how many chaos points you've got.

!shop

List everything currently on sale.

!info <command>

Full details on one item — cost, cooldown, and effect.

!buy <command> [text]

Purchase it. A few items (renames) take extra text after the command.

Mod / Broadcaster !give <user> <amount>

Grant someone chaos points out of thin air.

Mod / Broadcaster !setpoints <user> <amount>

Set someone's balance to an exact number.

How Chaos Points Work

Points build up automatically just from being part of the stream — no purchase required to start earning.

Doing this…EarnsNotes
Sitting in chat+10 ptsEvery 5 minutes, automatically, scaled by your sub-tier multiplier below.
Sending a message+5 ptsFlat rate, once per 60 seconds per person.
Following the channel+25 ptsOne-time.
Subscribing+100 ptsOne-time, and raises your passive-earning multiplier for as long as you're subbed.
Gifting a sub+50 ptsPer sub gifted, paid to the gifter.
Cheering+1 ptPer bit.
Leading a raid+2 ptsPer viewer you bring along.
Arriving in a raid+15 ptsPlus a first-message bonus that scales with raid size: +25 / +50 / +100 / +200 for small, medium, large, and massive raids.

Sub-tier multiplier — applies to the 5-minute passive tick only:

No sub ×1.0 Prime ×1.75 Tier 1 ×1.5 Tier 2 ×2.0 Tier 3 ×3.0

Yes — Prime actually out-earns Tier 1. Not a typo, just how the ladder is tuned.

Cooldowns are shared, not personal. Once anyone buys a command, it's locked for everyone until its cooldown runs out — you're competing with the rest of chat for the good stuff.

Raids do more than pay points. Every raid also rolls a random Chaos, Blessing, or Meta event on the farm — bigger raids roll better odds at the good stuff, and a Meta event can temporarily double everyone's point gain or cut every shop price in half.
